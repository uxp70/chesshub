    Bot ratings

    Elo is calibrated to feel like Chess.com bots: lower bots blunder and play randomly, higher bots search deeper. All run 100% in your browser — no server.

    How elo works here

    • Depth: 1 ply at 300–600, 2 at 900, 3 at 1200, 4 at 1500, 5 at 1850 (iterative deepening)
    • Randomness: ±250cp at 300 falling to ±5cp at 1850
    • Blunders: 26% random moves at 300 falling to 1% at 1850
    • Search: alpha-beta with transposition table, killer moves, history heuristic and quiescence
